## Declared G-R1 assumptions

1. Ordinary finite-dimensional quantum mechanics, Born probabilities,
   and a physical clock/system tensor split are inputs.
2. Complementary evenly spaced local spectra with gap epsilon>0 are
   chosen; both local Hamiltonians are nonnegative.
3. A fixed-total-energy constraint and matched joint state are postulated.
   The constraint is not derived from gravity or from Ei/Ed.
4. One covariant clock phase measurement is fixed before computing
   conditional probabilities. It is not tuned separately for each outcome.
5. The three-level uniform example and a four-level nonuniform example
   use model energy units, not measured masses or a physical clock scale.
6. The ideal clock/system interaction vanishes. A general interaction
   projection is derived only to identify what a completion must solve.
7. Only one-event conditional probabilities are certified. Repeated
   measurements require records and backreaction; a cyclic dial alone
   supplies neither an unbounded event counter nor an arrow of time.

## What is actually proved

- A positive-energy finite model admits a stationary joint state with
  exactly unitary, clock-conditioned subsystem evolution.
- The finite clock has a d-outcome orthogonal grid and a continuous
  covariant POVM. It has no exact canonical time/energy commutator.
- Gauge-averaged joint effects commute with the constraint and retain
  the same physical probabilities.
- Identical local marginals and complete joint energy statistics do
  not determine clock-conditioned coherent histories.
- Consequently, **ordinary energy density alone is insufficient**.
  This does not refute a richer Ei/Ed that includes coherences and
  additional dynamics, and does not prove that density slows time.
