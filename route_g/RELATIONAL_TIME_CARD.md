## What is actually proved

- A positive-energy finite model admits a stationary joint state with
  exactly unitary, clock-conditioned subsystem evolution.
- The finite clock has a d-outcome orthogonal grid and a continuous
  covariant POVM. It has no exact canonical time/energy commutator.
- Gauge-averaged joint effects commute with the constraint and retain
  the same physical probabilities.
- Identical local marginals and complete joint energy statistics do
  not determine clock-conditioned coherent histories.
- Consequently, **ordinary energy density alone is insufficient**.
  This does not refute a richer Ei/Ed that includes coherences and
  additional dynamics, and does not prove that density slows time.
