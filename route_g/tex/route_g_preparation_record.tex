\begin{abstract}
A relative-phase record can be written without changing the full system
energy distribution. We construct an explicit 72-dimensional unitary,
retain failed preparations, and calculate the measurement disturbance.
This adds a collective apparatus coupling to the unchanged G-R10 system;
it does not derive that coupling from the vertex contact. The same gate
erases its record if left active. Energy compatibility therefore closes
one finite instrument-existence question, not autonomous time generation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Unchanged system; only the relative reading is recorded}
In dimensionless units, keep the two-qutrit Hamiltonian at $\theta=\pi/2$:
\begin{align}
 L&=(2-\sqrt3)I+\sqrt3 N,\qquad N=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,2),\\
 H&=L\otimes I+I\otimes L+\gamma P_v\otimes P_v,
 \quad v=(1,1,1)^T/\sqrt3,\quad P_v=vv^\dagger.\label{H}
\end{align}
The inherited fixtures are $\gamma=0.09,0.03,0.01$, not physical fits.
Put $D=(\ket{D_+},\ket{D_-})$, $P=DD^\dagger$, $Q=I-P$, where
\begin{equation}
 \ket{D_+}=\frac{\ket{02}-2\ket{11}+\ket{20}}{\sqrt6},\qquad
 \ket{D_-}=\frac{\ket{02}-\ket{20}}{\sqrt2},\qquad HP=4P.
\end{equation}
These states are annihilated by the contact. For G-R10's fixed effects
$E_r=w_rw_r^\dagger/7$, $w_r=(1,e^{\ii\phi_r},e^{2\ii\phi_r})^T$,
$\phi_r=2\pi r/7$, define $\bar F_{rs}=\sum_E\Pi_E(E_r\otimes E_s)\Pi_E$.
This energy averaging is an operator definition, not a supplied clock.
For $d=(s-r)\bmod7$, $\delta_d=2\pi d/7$, direct projection gives
\begin{align}
 D^\dagger(w_r\otimes w_s)&=e^{\ii(\phi_r+\phi_s)}g_d,
 &g_d&=\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt{2/3}(\cos\delta_d-1)\\
                  \ii\sqrt2\sin\delta_d\end{pmatrix},\\
 C_d&=D\frac{g_dg_d^\dagger}{7}D^\dagger,
 &P\bar F_{r,r+d}P&=\frac{C_d}{7},\quad
 \sum_{d=0}^6C_d=P,\quad C_0=0.\label{compress}
\end{align}
Thus $r$ is uniformly redundant for \emph{every} state in $P$.
The reduced joint L\"uders instrument is also preserved by discarding $r$,
since $\sqrt{C_d/7}=\sqrt{C_d}/\sqrt7$. This is not the instrument of
separate local A/B phase measurements, nor a pair of absolute timestamps.

\section{A complete instrument and an explicit conserving unitary}
Include failure rather than presuming a free successful preparation:
\begin{equation}
 K_\perp=Q,\qquad K_d=\sqrt{C_d}\quad(d=1,\ldots,6),\qquad
 \sum_\ell K_\ell^\dagger K_\ell=I,\qquad [H,K_\ell]=0.\label{K}
\end{equation}
For $d\ne0$ the rank-one square root is elementary:
\begin{equation}
 K_d=D\frac{g_dg_d^\dagger/7}{\sqrt{g_d^\dagger g_d/7}}D^\dagger.
\end{equation}
The dark projection and relative readout can therefore be one operation.
It prepares a dark state on success, but not an undisturbed $D_+$.

Use one memory with eight orthogonal, energetically degenerate labels:
$\ket b,\ket\perp,\ket1,\ldots,\ket6$ and $H_M=0$.
The initially pure blank $b$ is distinct from failure; no hidden epoch
bit supplies that distinction. Eight is a minimum only for these seven
distinguishable outputs plus a separate blank in this register scheme.
Define the $72\times9$ embeddings
\begin{equation}
 J=I\otimes\ket b,\qquad
 W=Q\otimes\ket\perp+\sum_{d=1}^6 K_d\otimes\ket d.
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{K} gives $J^\dagger J=W^\dagger W=I$ and $J^\dagger W=0$.
With $P_J=JJ^\dagger$, $P_W=WW^\dagger$, construct
\begin{align}
 A&=WJ^\dagger-JW^\dagger,\quad G=\ii A=G^\dagger,\quad
 A^2=-(P_J+P_W),\\
 U&=I-P_J-P_W+A=\exp[-\ii(\pi/2)G].\label{U}
\end{align}
Indeed, $UJ=W$, $UW=-J$, and $U$ is the identity on the orthogonal
complement. These relations prove unitarity without an unspecified
extension. Since $H_{SM}J=JH$ and $H_{SM}W=WH$, where
$H_{SM}=H\otimes I_M$, they also prove
\begin{equation}
 [G,H_{SM}]=[U,H_{SM}]=0.\label{conserve}
\end{equation}
Thus the instrument preserves the entire distribution of $H$, not just
its mean. Measuring a memory projector produces the corresponding Kraus
branch. Before that measurement the joint state need not be classical.

\paragraph{What has been added.}
$G$ is a new \emph{collective} system--memory interaction, not a consequence
of $\gamma P_v\otimes P_v$ or a demonstrated local hardware coupling.
For a controlled Hamiltonian $H_{SM}+\kappa(\tau)G$ and pulse area
$a=\int\kappa(\tau)d\tau$, the interaction-picture gate is $e^{-\ii aG}$.
The physical propagator also contains $e^{-\ii H_{SM}\tau}$.
This factor leaves all record probabilities and energy statistics below
unchanged, but must be retained for subsequent coherent experiments.
Here $\tau$ and pulse control are assumed, not a newly derived time.

\section{All outcomes, their energies, and the measurement disturbance}
For the declared input $\ket{11}$,
\begin{equation}
 P\ket{11}=-\sqrt{2/3}\ket{D_+},\quad
 Q\ket{11}=\ket B/\sqrt3,\quad
 \ket B=(\ket{02}+\ket{11}+\ket{20})/\sqrt3.
\end{equation}
The failure probability is $1/3$; success totals $2/3$. Conditional on
success the relative record has the normalized distribution
\begin{equation}
 q_d=\bra{D_+}C_d\ket{D_+}
 =\frac{2}{21}(1-\cos\delta_d)^2,\qquad
 p_\perp=\frac13,\quad p_d=\frac23q_d.\label{probs}
\end{equation}
The finite Fourier sums give $\sum_d\cos\delta_d=0$ and
$\sum_d\cos^2\delta_d=7/2$, proving normalization. Numerically:

\midrule
Probability & $0.333333333$ & $0.009000630$ & $0.094892536$ / $0.229440167$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The complete energy ledger, including rejected preparations, is
\begin{equation}
 E_{\rm in}=4+\frac\gamma9,\quad E_S=4,\quad
 E_\perp=4+\frac\gamma3,\quad
 \frac23E_S+\frac13E_\perp=E_{\rm in}.\label{ledger}
\end{equation}
At $\gamma=0.09$ these are $4.01$, $4$, and $4.03$. $B$ is the failure
state for $U$ in the interaction picture, not generally an eigenstate
of $H$; its free evolution changes neither the failure probability nor
its energy distribution. Success lies in the stationary eigenspace $P$.

\paragraph{Non-negligible backreaction.}
Each successful record yields a pure dark state proportional to
$Dg_d$. If $d$ is unread after success, the system becomes exactly
\begin{equation}
 \rho'_S=\sum_{d=1}^6K_d\ket{D_+}\bra{D_+}K_d
 =\frac{57}{82}\ket{D_+}\bra{D_+}
  +\frac{25}{82}\ket{D_-}\bra{D_-}.\label{backreaction}
\end{equation}
This is an analytic ratio, not a decimal fit. To verify it, put
$c_d=\cos(2\pi d/7)$. Pairing $d$ with $7-d$ cancels the off-diagonal
term. The $D_+$ weight is
\begin{align}
 F&=\frac1{21}\sum_{d=0}^6\frac{(1-c_d)^3}{2+c_d}
   =-\frac92+\frac97\sum_{d=0}^6\frac1{2+c_d},\\
 \sum_d\frac1{2+c_d}
 &=-\frac{T'_7(-2)}{T_7(-2)-1}=\frac{497}{123},\qquad F=\frac{57}{82}.
\end{align}
Here $T_7(z)-1$ has roots $c_d$ counted with multiplicity;
$T_7(-2)=-5042$, $T'_7(-2)=20377$. Every successful branch and their
mixture retain G-R10's conditional orbit
$\rho_{B|\phi}=e^{\ii N_B\phi}\rho_{B|0}e^{-\ii N_B\phi}$,
but with a changed seed state. This does not mean $D_+$ survives unchanged.

\section{Which phase reference is avoided, and why a record can disappear}
An elementary covariance argument makes the resource boundary precise.
Let an apparatus have Hamiltonian $H_R$, state $\sigma_R$, pointer $Z_\ell$,
and joint unitary $V$. Suppose
\begin{equation}
 [\sigma_R,H_R]=[Z_\ell,H_R]=0,\qquad
 [V,H\otimes I+I\otimes H_R]=0.
\end{equation}
Its outcome probability and induced effect are
\begin{equation}
 p_\ell(\rho)=\tr[V(\rho\otimes\sigma_R)V^\dagger(I\otimes Z_\ell)]
             =\tr(\rho F_\ell).
\end{equation}
Write $T_t=e^{-\ii Ht}$ and $R_t=e^{-\ii H_Rt}$. Invariance of
$\sigma_R$ gives
$(T_t\rho T_t^\dagger)\otimes\sigma_R
 =(T_t\otimes R_t)(\rho\otimes\sigma_R)(T_t\otimes R_t)^\dagger$.
Commuting this joint translation through $V$ and the pointer, then
using trace cyclicity, proves
\begin{equation}
 p_\ell(T_t\rho T_t^\dagger)=p_\ell(\rho)\quad\hbox{for every }\rho,
 \qquad [F_\ell,H]=0.\label{reference}
\end{equation}
The relative effects $C_d$ meet this condition, while the bare local
phase effects $E_r\otimes I$ generally do not. For example, the inherited
fixtures all give $\|[H,E_0\otimes I]\|_{\rm F}\simeq1.484615$.
Thus our explicit $U$ needs no external \emph{energy-time phase asymmetry}
resource, but does not implement the separate local phase POVM using
such invariant resources. Internal relative calibration, pure blank
memory, and collective control are still inputs. This is a direct finite
covariance proof, consistent with the broader WAY literature~\cite{way};
it is not a new general measurement theorem.

\paragraph{Record versus autonomous event.}
Using $AJ=W$, $AW=-J$, the continuously active gate obeys
\begin{equation}
 e^{-\ii aG}J=\cos a\,J+\sin a\,W,
 \qquad P_{\rm blank}(a)=\cos^2a.\label{blank}
\end{equation}
At $a=\pi/2$ a record exists. At $a=\pi$ the entire input is blank again,
up to sign: $U^2J=-J$. Including free system evolution changes that
system vector but not its blank-memory status. Therefore a static
energy-conserving gate is not, by itself, a durable autonomous recorder.

If the coupling is explicitly turned off after writing, subsequent
$e^{-\ii Ht}\otimes I_M$ preserves every memory label. Its value can
be reread without rerunning the system POVM; the latter is nonprojective
and need not yield the same $d$ again. Actual isolation, noise and
lifetime are not modeled. The premeasurement may also retain coherence
between labels, so neither irreversible classicalization nor a universal
arrow follows from the unitary. The missing physical choice is a
trigger and decoupling mechanism, not a smaller numerical error.

\section{Information ledger, verification and bounded conclusion}
For a \emph{classicalized} record the Shannon entropy, in bits, is
\begin{align}
 H_{\rm rec}&=h_2(2/3)+\frac23H(q)
       =2.27003421247,\qquad H(q)=2.02760756762,\\
 H_{\rm redundant}&=\frac23\log_2 7=1.87156994804.
\end{align}
The second number is the per-attempt information cost of also saving
the uniformly redundant common-origin label $r$. Removing it loses no
dark-sector relative instrument, by Eq.~\eqref{compress}; it does not
remove failure or selectively discard unfavorable records.

For ideal thermal resetting of a degenerate classical register at
temperature $T$, no accessible side information, and cyclic controls,
the free-energy bound is~\cite{landauer}
\begin{equation}
 W_{\rm reset}\ge k_BT\ln2\,H_{\rm rec}.
\end{equation}
This is a conditional reset bound, not a universal writing cost or an
actual heat prediction. A coherent register, accessible system--memory
correlations, a finite bath, or a noncyclic controller require their own
ledger. No kelvin/joule calibration is supplied. $H_M=0$ and conservation
of bare energy do not make pure blank preparation, control, fabrication
or resetting free. The apparatus pulse has not been embedded into an
autonomous energy source.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The bounded energy-compatible instrument-existence task closes here.
One may keep this as an effective relational-clock measurement with
explicit control assumptions. If the aim remains autonomous event/time
generation, next choose a concrete physical trigger and self-decoupling
mechanism and account for its energy and records. Do not add another
abstract measurement prerequisite, bath, fitted time law or parameter
scan as a substitute for that choice. This construction proves neither
the source paper's $E_d$ law nor emergent seconds, spacetime, particle
masses, or any Route-F promotion.
