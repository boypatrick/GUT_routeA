\begin{abstract}
The extra resonant channel found in G-R9 supports, rather than prevents,
an exact stationary dark subspace. Within it a subsystem's phase reading
labels another subsystem's state without the auxiliary update index.
All nine states and all readout outcomes are retained. However, the
original exchange input has only $2/3$ dark weight, conserved by this
contact: existence is not autonomous preparation. Generic stationary
interacting states also need not follow the bare-gap rigid clock orbit.
This is a finite conditional quantum construction, not a derivation of
physical seconds, records, an arrow, spacetime or particle masses.
\end{abstract}

\section{Same contact and a complete observation algebra}
Keep G-R9's two independent three-level copies. At its already declared
resonance $\theta=\pi/2$, in the fixed Fourier-mode basis,
\begin{equation}
 L=\lambda_0 I+\Delta N,\quad N=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,2),\quad
 \lambda_0=2-\sqrt3,\quad\Delta=\sqrt3.
\end{equation}
The full dimensionless generator is unchanged:
\begin{equation}
 H=L\otimes I+I\otimes L+\gamma P_v\otimes P_v,
 \quad v=\frac{(1,1,1)^T}{\sqrt3},\quad P_v=vv^\dagger.\label{H}
\end{equation}
Here $P_v$ is one vertex projector expressed in mode coordinates; no
outlet is removed. $\gamma=0.09,0.03,0.01$ are inherited fixtures, not fits.

The Hermitian local basis consists of $Z_k=\ket k\bra k$ and, for $k<l$,
\begin{equation}
 X_{kl}=\ket k\bra l+\ket l\bra k,\qquad
 Y_{kl}=-\ii\ket k\bra l+\ii\ket l\bra k.
\end{equation}
These nine matrices span the local algebra; their 81 products span the
joint algebra. Incompatible settings require separate runs. For the
clock readout fix seven effects before choosing a state:
\begin{equation}
 \phi_r=2\pi r/7,\quad w(\phi)=(1,e^{\ii\phi},e^{2\ii\phi})^T,
 \quad E_r=\frac{w(\phi_r)w(\phi_r)^\dagger}{7},\quad
 \sum_{r=0}^6 E_r=I.\label{POVM}
\end{equation}
Completeness follows from the finite Fourier sum for differences
$k-l\in\{-2,\ldots,2\}$. Positivity is immediate. The phase effects
alone span only five local dimensions: this readout is not tomography.

\section{Joint probabilities, unobserved outcomes and a clock criterion}
For any full nine-state density matrix, define
\begin{align}
 p_{rs}&=\tr[\rho(E_r\otimes E_s)],&
 R_B(r)&=\tr_A[(E_r\otimes I)\rho],\label{prob}\\
 p_r&=\tr R_B(r),&\rho_{B|r}&=R_B(r)/p_r.
\end{align}
For $p_r>0$ this is the remote conditional state for any local instrument
on A with effect $E_r$; cyclicity of the partial trace removes its
particular Kraus representation. It says nothing about a later A
measurement or the apparatus energy. Without an A record,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_r p_r\rho_{B|r}=\tr_A\rho.\label{unread}
\end{equation}
No preferred exit is postselected. The continuous extension has effects
$w(\phi)w(\phi)^\dagger\,d\phi/(2\pi)$; it is a POVM, not an infinity
of orthogonal timestamps.

For the resonant triple, write
\begin{equation}
 \ket\psi=c_0\ket{0,2}+c_1\ket{1,1}+c_2\ket{2,0},\quad
 \sum_k|c_k|^2=1.
\end{equation}
All phase marginals are uniform, $p_r=1/7$, and the normalized B vector is
\begin{equation}
 \ket{\psi_B(\phi)}=\sum_{k=0}^2c_k e^{-\ii k\phi}\ket{2-k}
 =e^{-2\ii\phi}e^{\ii N_B\phi}\ket{\psi_B(0)}.\label{orbit}
\end{equation}
Thus $\rho_{B|\phi}=e^{\ii N_B\phi}\rho_{B|0}e^{-\ii N_B\phi}$.
The same conclusion holds for mixed states supported on this triple.
This is an exact conditional orbit, but a generic triple state is not
stationary under the full contact. The next section identifies states
that satisfy both requirements.

For the weak-contact exchange approximation from $\ket{1,1}$,
$c_0=c_2=a=(z-1)/3$, $c_1=b=(z+2)/3$, $z=e^{-\ii x}$,
$x=\gamma s/3$, $s=\alpha n$, $\alpha=1/4$. All three amplitudes give
\begin{equation}
 p_{rs}=\frac{|b+2a\cos(\phi_r-\phi_s)|^2}{49}.\label{tripleprob}
\end{equation}
The relative-phase harmonics are $C_1=-|a|^2$ and $C_2=|a|^2$.
Correlations change with the assumed exchange index through $a,b$;
Eq.~\eqref{orbit} alone has not eliminated this preparation dependence.

We test the full contact against this \emph{specified bare-gap} orbit:
\begin{equation}
 D_7(\rho)=\max_{r=0,\ldots,6}\frac12
 \left\|\rho_{B|r}-e^{\ii N_B\phi_r}\rho_{B|0}e^{-\ii N_B\phi_r}\right\|_1.
 \label{D7}
\end{equation}
A nonzero value rejects that rigid description on the actual seven
readings, not every possible interacting-clock interpretation.

\section{An exact dark clock, and an exact preparation obstruction}
The triple's free total eigenvalue is 4. The contact has every
mode-basis element $\gamma/9$, so a triple vector is annihilated iff
$c_0+c_1+c_2=0$. An orthonormal basis is
\begin{equation}
 \ket{D_+}=\frac{\ket{0,2}-2\ket{1,1}+\ket{2,0}}{\sqrt6},\qquad
 \ket{D_-}=\frac{\ket{0,2}-\ket{2,0}}{\sqrt2}.\label{dark}
\end{equation}
For every $\gamma$ in Eq.~\eqref{H}, not just a weak-coupling limit,
\begin{equation}
 V\ket{D_\pm}=0,\qquad H\ket{D_\pm}=4\ket{D_\pm}.
\end{equation}
Consequently every density matrix supported on this dark subspace is
stationary and has the conditional orbit in Eq.~\eqref{orbit}.
No update number occurs in that readout relation. Defining a formal
dial orientation $\xi=-\phi$ gives
$\ii\partial_\xi\rho_{B|\xi}=[N_B,\rho_{B|\xi}]$.
Its unit slope follows from the chosen equal-gap spectrum; it is not
a prediction of a universal physical time rate.

For $D_+$ the continuous relative-phase density, $\delta=\phi_A-\phi_B$, is
\begin{equation}
 p(\delta)=\frac{(1-\cos\delta)^2}{3\pi},\qquad
 C_1=-\frac23,\quad C_2=\frac16.\label{darkread}
\end{equation}
It is broad and peaks at a fixed offset $\delta=\pi$; equal phase
outcomes have zero joint probability. Ignoring A gives diagonal
$\rho_B=\operatorname{diag}(1,4,1)/6$ and a uniform B phase distribution.
Local mode dephasing preserves all energy populations and both reduced
states but makes the joint phase table flat. This is a coherence
control, not a unique experimental entanglement witness. The dephased
control need not itself remain stationary under $H$.

$D_-$ instead gives $p(\delta)=\sin^2\delta/\pi$, with a twofold
ambiguity in the continuous dial. Identical copies keep $D_-$ an exact
eigenstate even off the equal-gap resonance: the two exchanged levels
always have equal total energy. $D_+$ loses stationarity off resonance.
This robustness tradeoff must not be concealed by retuning a clock.

Let $P_D=\ket{D_+}\bra{D_+}+\ket{D_-}\bra{D_-}$. The full contact obeys
\begin{equation}
 [H,P_D]=0,\qquad\bra{1,1}P_D\ket{1,1}=\frac23,\qquad
 |\bra{D_+}e^{-\ii\alpha nH}\ket{1,1}|^2=\frac23.\label{prep}
\end{equation}
The antisymmetric overlap remains zero. Thus the original unitary
exchange cannot prepare a pure dark clock. A hypothetical dark-energy
filter would succeed with probability $2/3$ and reject $1/3$; its
instrument, energy accounting and records would be new inputs.
The dark clock also has zero contact action: persistent correlations
are not proof of ongoing energy exchange or clock synchronization.

\section{Full stationary readout and the retained interaction term}
For a normalized full eigenstate $H\ket\Psi=E\ket\Psi$, set
$q_\phi=w(\phi)/\sqrt3$ and the unnormalized conditional amplitude
$\chi(\phi)=(\bra{q_\phi}\otimes I)\ket\Psi$. Since
$\bra{q_\phi}N_A\ket\Psi=\ii\partial_\phi\chi$ and $q_0=v$,
\begin{equation}
 \ii\Delta\partial_\phi\chi(\phi)
 =(E-2\lambda_0-\Delta N_B)\chi(\phi)
 -\gamma f(\phi)P_v\chi(0),\quad
 f(\phi)=\frac{1+e^{-\ii\phi}+e^{-2\ii\phi}}3.\label{kernel}
\end{equation}
This is exact on all nine states. The contact couples different dial
readings, rather than merely rescaling time. For a dark state
$P_v\chi(0)=0$ and $E-2\lambda_0=2\Delta$, yielding Eq.~\eqref{orbit}.
For generic eigenstates the term survives and $\|\chi(\phi)\|$ can vary.
Normalizing it without retaining that likelihood would give a wrong
conditional equation. Conditional dynamics from stationary correlations
is established Page--Wootters reasoning~\cite{pw}; interactions can
produce nonlocal clock-label equations~\cite{smith}. The specific
finite contact kernel above is derived here, not a new general mechanism.

\paragraph{An unread update is a specified prior, not an automatic law.}
Let $U=e^{-\ii\alpha H}$ and assume the uniform Cesaro average
\begin{equation}
 \bar\rho=\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac1T\sum_{n=0}^{T-1}U^n\rho_0U^{-n}
 =\sum_E\Pi_E\rho_0\Pi_E,\qquad\rho_0=\ket{1,1}\bra{1,1}.\label{avg}
\end{equation}
For the declared fixtures $\alpha(E_{\max}-E_{\min})<2\pi$, so there
are no distinct-energy update-phase aliases. The equality follows
by summing each off-diagonal geometric series. This prior, not an
arbitrary unknown distribution of $n$, gives $[H,\bar\rho]=0$.
It need not give $[N_A+N_B,\bar\rho]=0$ or $D_7=0$.

Stationary effects can also be defined without deleting outcomes:
\begin{equation}
 \bar F_{rs}=\sum_E\Pi_E(E_r\otimes E_s)\Pi_E,\qquad
 \sum_{rs}\bar F_{rs}=I,\quad[H,\bar F_{rs}]=0.\label{effects}
\end{equation}
They are positive and give the same probabilities as $E_r\otimes E_s$
on stationary $\rho$. They are generally \emph{joint}, not local
product effects. This logical POVM does not establish an autonomous,
energy-conserving, locally implemented recording apparatus.

\paragraph{Order of limits matters.}
Writing $\ket B=(\ket{0,2}+\ket{1,1}+\ket{2,0})/\sqrt3$, the
positive weak-coupling limit after Eq.~\eqref{avg} is
\begin{equation}
 \bar\rho_{0^+}=\frac23\ket{D_+}\bra{D_+}+\frac13\ket B\bra B.
\end{equation}
The bright eigenvector tends to $B$, the dark weight stays $2/3$,
and other multiplet overlaps vanish. This mixed state has
$(C_1,C_2)=(-2/9,2/9)$ and a rigid conditional orbit. Setting $\gamma=0$
\emph{before} averaging instead leaves $\rho_0$, with flat phase
statistics. Weak coupling is not instantaneous dark-state preparation.
