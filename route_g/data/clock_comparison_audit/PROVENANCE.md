# G-R5: acquired clock data and fitness for the thermal test

Accessed: 2026-09-24. No experimental values have been invented or fitted.

## What was obtained

1. R. Lange et al., PTB dataset,
   [DOI 10.7795/720.20211028](https://oar.ptb.de/resources/show/10.7795/720.20211028),
   related to [PRL126,011102(2021)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.126.011102).
   The complete 2.8 KB archive is preserved as `ptb_lpi_2021.zip`.
   CC BY 4.0; creator attribution and original README remain inside.
   Download URL: https://oar.ptb.de/files/download/681c535c53218de84f08e868
   SHA256: `3f38135ebfab1375391195c282aa1980a76657ba9125b59e6e1cb85695a400e6`.
   It contains 11 E3/E2 comparison points (MJD57527--59081), not a
   paired two-temperature experiment. Its ratio offset is an absolute
   ratio difference, not a fractional difference. The associated paper
   uses two separate clock systems in this campaign.

2. T. Lindvall, M. Pizzocaro, R. Godun et al.,
   [ROCIT dataset, DOI10.5281/zenodo.17107693](https://zenodo.org/records/17107693),
   published 2025-09-12, for the 2022 campaign described in
   [Optica12,843(2025)](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.561754).
   CC BY 4.0 verified via https://zenodo.org/api/records/17107693.
   Full archive downloaded and checksum checked:
   https://zenodo.org/records/17107693/files/ROCIT%20campaign%20results.zip?download=1
   MD5: `4ae290f559c90b462991286c933a1147` (publisher).
   SHA256: `6168e24a0c29ce0929e9651460f11ea77f151176e1ba3d0fe3428e1e08bd56bd`.
   All 34 files of the selected PTB E2/E3 comparator were audited:
   2,324,201 rows, MJD59632.371875--59674.958218, 2,324,201 marked valid,
   zero nonfinite numeric values and zero duplicate timestamps.
   The exact first day's 51,990 rows plus its YAML metadata are preserved
   under `rocit_2025/`; no values or line endings were changed.
   Other days remain obtainable from the pinned release, not duplicated
   in the repository. Full audit includes every selected member hash.

These are original release files, **not necessarily uncorrected atom/servo
records**. The ROCIT comparator was generated by upstream processing
script `06-procclocks-v3.py`. Its five columns are MJD, comparator output,
validity, and two systematic uncertainties. All are valid by the release's
nonzero-flag rule; this is not our certification of independent samples.
The release and paper warn of network inconsistencies. We perform no
new significance, thermal-slope or common-time fit.

## Why the requested thermal test remains open

No selected release provides the paired temperature-state labels,
independent radiation spectrum/temperature calibration at the ion,
or per-run BBR correction ledger needed to reconstruct an uncorrected
two-state response. Metadata contains nominal clock frequencies and
scaling, not those thermal channels. Good row integrity does not fix
this missing experimental design. This is an eligibility limitation,
not an allegation of bad data or a proof that such data exist nowhere.

The 2025 paper says data were available on request at publication; the
later September2025 public deposit supersedes that general access claim.
Do not repeat the paper's older access statement as current.

The [format specification](https://github.com/INRIM/optical-link-data-format)
defines Delta_A_to_B=(nu_B-rho0_BA nu_A)/sB. For this comparator A=E3,
B=E2, the reverse of Route-G's preferred E3/E2 ratio. To recover nu_B/nu_A
exactly requires the reference convention/nu_A in the same units, not
merely negating the released column. No approximate conversion is used
for a physical fit here. Current format treats flag1 as valid-experimental;
the release explicitly specifies nonzero as valid.

## Other evidence checked, not substituted

- [Filzinger et al., PRL130,253001(2023)](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.03433v1)
  establishes same-ion interleaved E3/E2 and an external Sr comparison,
  but its clock-comparison discussion does not supply the missing
  paired bath calibration for our test. No graph was digitized as raw data.
- [Tofful et al., Metrologia61,045001(2024)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1681-7575/ad53cd)
  gives apparatus/systematic information, not the paired raw E2/E3
  thermal experiment. Sideband thermometry measures ion motion; it
  cannot stand in for radiation temperature at the ion.
- [Steinel et al., PRL131,083002(2023)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.083002)
  supplies a relevant external-reference precedent, using Sr+ versus
  Yb+ E3 and inferring thermal conditions spectroscopically. It is not
  the requested same-ion E2/E3 data; inferring a bath from the tested
  frequency law cannot independently validate that same law.

## Reproduce

    python3 route_g/code/audit_gr5_clock_data.py
    python3 route_g/code/audit_gr5_clock_data.py --rocit-zip /path/to/downloaded.zip

The first command audits the bundled excerpt; the second verifies the
complete archive hash and audits all selected days. Their output files
are distinct. Missing thermal data remain missing, never zero-filled.
