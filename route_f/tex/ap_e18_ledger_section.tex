\section{AP-E18: GTA instability and the scale-polished gate}

AP-E18 tests whether AP-E17's graph-tight annulus condition follows from the
actual minimizing diagonal.  The decisive distinction is between additive
near-minimality on the tangent ball and a comparison gauge uniform over all
unresolved subballs.  AP-E17 supplies only
\[
 \epsilon_{j_\ell}(B_{\rho r_\ell})=o(r_\ell^3)
 \qquad(0<\rho<1\text{ fixed}),
\]
which gives no control of
$\epsilon_{j_\ell}(B_s)/s^3$ when $s/r_\ell\to0$.

The standard reverse-H\"older imports do not close this gap.  The ambient
growth exponents are $(p,q)=(2,6)$, outside the
$q<\min\{np/(n-1),p+1\}=3$ range in dimension three.  Although
\[
 \Xi(Du)=(Du,\sqrt R\,M_2(Du),\sqrt K\,M_3(Du))
\]
obeys curl/div identities and has quadratic energy, it is not a free linear
$\mathscr A$-field: the Pl\"ucker identities, $|u|=1$, and $u^TDu=0$ remain
nonlinear constraints.  Minimality is not available in the enlarged
$\mathscr A$-free class.

The exact obstruction is a rank-two needle straddling the tangent-ball
boundary.  With
\[
 A_\varepsilon=\varepsilon^{-1/2},\qquad
 b_\varepsilon=h_\varepsilon=\varepsilon^2,
\]
its strong tangent $L^2$ mass is $O(\varepsilon^5)$ and both its leading
first- and second-minor energies are $O(\varepsilon)$, while
\[
 \int|z_\varepsilon|^2|Dz_\varepsilon|^2\asymp1,\qquad
 \int|z_\varepsilon|^2|M_2(Dz_\varepsilon)|^2\asymp1.
\]
For every annulus thickness $\delta_\varepsilon\downarrow0$, the GTA
quotient is bounded below by
\[
 c\delta_\varepsilon^{-2}\quad(\delta_\varepsilon\geq h_\varepsilon),
 \qquad
 \frac{c}{\varepsilon^2\delta_\varepsilon}
 \quad(\delta_\varepsilon<h_\varepsilon).
\]
Both diverge, even though the annular graph energy tends to zero.

At physical radius $r_\varepsilon=\varepsilon$, the chart displacement is
$\sqrt\varepsilon$ and the extra energy is
$O(r_\varepsilon^4)=o(r_\varepsilon^3)$.  A compact chart homotopy preserves
degree and the perturbation is invisible to the tangent defect measure.
Consequently GTA is not a stable consequence of the present additive
diagonal axioms.  This is not a proof that no specially selected recovery
sequence has GTA.

The replacement mainline is a scale-polished recovery sequence satisfying,
down to a declared cutoff scale,
\[
 \epsilon_\ell(B_s(y))\leq\omega_\ell s^3,
 \qquad\omega_\ell\to0,
\]
or a direct proof of energy--amplitude tightness
\[
 \int|v_\ell-v_0|^2
 \{1+R|Dv_\ell|^2+K|M_2(Dv_\ell)|^2\}\to0
\]
together with a one-hemisphere collar.  Existence of such a diagonal remains
open.  The AP-E18 continuum card passes $14/14$; full $\mu=0$, classicality,
isolation, Hessian, determinant, and portal promotion remain closed.
